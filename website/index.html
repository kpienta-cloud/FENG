About this project

This graph was assembled for the 3rd Annual Feng Symposium keynote, “Transformative Science and the Big Questions Shaping the Future of Prostate Cancer,” delivered by Kenneth Pienta, MD. Publication data was pulled directly from the National Library of Medicine's PubMed database using the E-utilities API, searching for “Feng FY[Full Author Name]” and filtering to English-language records with affiliations or subject matter matching radiation oncology, genitourinary oncology, or prostate cancer, to exclude unrelated authors who share the same name and initials.

Research themes were assigned by matching each paper's title, abstract, MeSH terms, and keywords against a curated list of eighteen terms spanning molecular discovery (SChLAP1, PCAT1, TMPRSS2-ERG, androgen receptor biology), precision medicine (the Decipher genomic classifier, circulating tumor DNA), and clinical translation (salvage radiotherapy, castration-resistant disease, immunotherapy and gene therapy, health disparities). A paper can and often does belong to more than one theme.

Source data, the extraction scripts, the full paper, and this site live in the project repository at github.com/kpienta-cloud/FENG.
